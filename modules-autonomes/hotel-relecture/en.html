Thank you for reviewing this. It takes about 5 hours: 647 short texts, 4454 words in all. Your work is saved in this browser: you can close the page and come back.

What this is. A training kit for front-desk employees of a hotel in Quebec. Some of them speak French and are learning English. They hear the English lines, answer guests in role-plays, and read the screen in their own language. The hotel, the guests and their names are invented.

What we need from you. For each text: does it sound like something a real person would say or read, in North American English, at a hotel front desk? Guests speak casually and fast; the receptionist is polite and professional. Screen texts should be short and plain.

Tricky words: the notes14 ·

TRAP: « une chambre double » is a room for two — one big bed or two beds. Ask: « un grand lit ou deux lits? »
TRAP: « le premier étage » can mean the ground floor (Québec) or the floor above it (France). « Le rez-de-chaussée » is always the ground floor.
TRAP: in Québec, « le déjeuner » is BREAKFAST. Lunch is « le dîner »; dinner is « le souper ».
TRAP: « le souper » is the evening meal. In Québec, « le dîner » is lunch.
TRAP: French « une caution » is a security deposit, not caution (« la prudence »).
TRAP: 04/05 is May 4 in Québec (day first), not April 5. Say the month in words.
TRAP: French « la location » means RENTAL. A place is « un endroit ».
TRAP: French « la monnaie » is CHANGE (the coins you give back). Money is « l'argent ».
TRAP: « un billet » is a ticket (or a banknote). A bill to pay is « la facture ».
TRAP: Spanish « éxito » means success, not exit. Exit is « la salida ».
TRAP: Spanish « actualmente » means right now, not « actually » (= « en realidad »).
TRAP: Spanish « estoy constipado » means I have a cold, not constipated.
TRAP: Spanish « embarazada » means pregnant. Embarrassed is « me da pena ».
TRAP: Spanish « carpeta » is a folder; a carpet is « una alfombra ».

At the desk: the rule1 ·

You decide alone: look up a booking, re-encode a key, explain a charge. The manager decides: a refund, a waived fee, anything free, any exception. And no one promises what the hotel can't give: a tax is never removed, a room you don't have is never promised, a false document (bill, date, payment method) is never made.
French original : Vous décidez seul : chercher une réservation, réencoder une clé, expliquer des frais. Le gérant décide : un remboursement, des frais effacés, un service gratuit, toute exception. Et personne ne promet ce que l'hôtel ne peut pas donner : une taxe ne s'enlève jamais, une chambre qu'on n'a pas ne se promet pas, un document faux (facture, date, mode de paiement) ne se fait jamais.
