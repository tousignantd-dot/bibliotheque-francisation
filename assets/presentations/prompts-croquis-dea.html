Prompts · 23 septembre 2026 · séquence de l'appareil

Cinq croquis à refaire ailleurs

Un prompt autonome par image : le style, le décor, les personnes, la caméra et les deux règles qui ont été ratées ici — une seule électrode visible, sous l'aisselle du côté de la caméra, l'autre cachée derrière son épaule droite ; les mains entre les deux mamelons, cinq doigts, bras tendus. En anglais : les modèles d'image y répondent mieux.

Ordre conseillé.
  • Faire d'abord a5-reprendre : c'est la référence des trois suivants.
  • Puis a5-reprendre-bas en ne lui donnant QUE le nouvel a5-reprendre — la paire doit être identique sauf la poitrine enfoncée.
  • b-relais et b-passation avec le nouvel a5-reprendre en référence, pour garder la même femme et le même trait.
  • Format 16:9, crayon clair. Déposer les images retenues dans simulateur-dea/croquis/gros-plans/ sous le même nom ; je reconstruis la formation et la simulation.

« Il analyse » — personne ne touche

No colour anywhere. Realistic proportions, calm and non-dramatic, like a first-aid training illustration. No text, letters, numbers, logos or brand names anywhere — not on the device, not on clothing, not on the electrode pads. 16:9 landscape.

CAMERA: medium shot from slightly above, at the height of a standing adult, from the side of the man lying on the floor. The man lies on his back ACROSS the frame, his feet at the left edge and his head to the RIGHT. We see him from his LEFT side.

THE MAN ON THE FLOOR: about 55 years old, grey hair, clean-shaven, eyes closed, face relaxed (unconscious, not in pain), a pale open-collar work shirt unbuttoned and pulled open so his chest is bare, dark work trousers with a belt, heavy boots. A real middle-aged chest: soft, a little grey hair, not muscular, not young.

THE WOMAN: about 40 years old, mid-length dark hair tied back in a low ponytail, a grey cardigan over a white top, a lanyard with an ID badge. She kneels on the FAR side of his chest, facing us. The defibrillator is analysing, so NOBODY TOUCHES HIM: she sits back on her heels with BOTH HANDS RAISED, palms open, lifted clear of his body, her eyes on the device.

THE DEFIBRILLATOR (AED): a pale, rounded plastic box the size of a small briefcase, with a moulded handle on top, a small dark screen showing a jagged heart-rhythm line, and one large round button below it marked only with a heart crossed by a lightning bolt. It stands upright on the floor on the NEAR side of his body, between him and the viewer, near his waist.

THE ELECTRODE PADS — the most important detail. EXACTLY ONE pad is visible: a large flat white rectangular pad, about the size of a hand, stuck on the SIDE of his chest on the side facing us, HIGH UP, directly below his armpit, on the ribs at the level of the nipple. It is NOT on the front of the chest and NOT on the belly. The SECOND pad is stuck below his RIGHT collarbone, on the FAR side of his chest, so the camera CANNOT see it: do not draw it. All we see of it is a thin cable disappearing over the top of his chest toward his far shoulder. Two thin cables in total run from his chest to the defibrillator. No small round ECG electrodes anywhere.

IN THE BACKGROUND: far away near the counter, a grey-haired man with glasses stands calmly talking on his phone, and a woman stands beside him. Both calm, hands away from their faces. Nobody else near the body.

AVOID: a second visible pad, pads side by side, pads on the belly, six fingers, hands below the nipple line, bent elbows, hands on the victim's face or shoulder, bystanders with hands over their mouths, dramatic lighting, heavy black shading, any text or numbers.
