The pilot: what you need to know

For each participant
  1. It's voluntary. You can stop at any time, without explaining why and without any consequence.
  2. You are not being evaluated. The material is: if many people miss the same question, the question is bad, and we fix it.
  3. You don't write your name. You enter with the code shown in class; you become "Participant 1, 2, 3…" for the session.
  4. What is recorded. Your answers to multiple-choice questions (right or not, on the first try), with no name. In the desk role-play: which skills you used, not your sentences.
  5. Your voice. In the speaking test, your recording stays on your phone; it is deleted once the trainer confirms your level, or after 30 days. In the desk role-play, the browser turns your voice into text (in Chrome, through a Google service); that text is sent to an artificial intelligence service that makes the guest answer, and is not kept.
  6. Your employer. They receive a report on the material and on the group. Never your name, never your results.
